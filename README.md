Entry for the make.fun bounty [Pigeon Pitch, One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf): one line, at most 200 characters, the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, offline, standard library only — no randomness, no network, no timestamps.

## Entry

> CrumbBox delivers fresh breadcrumbs in 4 minutes, by 2,000 pigeons, for $9 a month — and our couriers already ate your last order.
